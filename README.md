# Chez Bébé

A private place for two. Owner: Her Imperial Majesty. Guest: Abdulmalik (on probation).

Setup is in **SETUP.md**.

## What's inside

- **Home:** today at a glance. Both your days side by side (good, okay, rough), notes, the good morning and its royal reply, what's waiting for you, saving-up goals, and what's coming up.
- **Check-in:** how's your day, one line or a quick voice note, and one-tap notes written once in your own words for busy days.
- **Quiet days:** when either of you marks a rough day, banter pauses for them: no new cases, decrees or duel nudges. The other gets "No jokes. Just be there." and an **I'm here** button that softly lights up the other phone.
- **Court:** either of you files a case; the accused writes the defence; the accuser gives the verdict (guilty, pardon or dismiss) with an optional sentence. An all-time record shows who's always right.
- **Decrees:** official requests, carried out with one tap.
- **Duel:** the same Sudoku for both of you every day. Fastest wins, mistakes cost 10 seconds, and your time stays hidden from the other until they finish.
- **Board:** castles, houses, places and dreams, with photos and reel links. Any pin can become a goal.
- **Crowns and goals:** everything you do earns crowns, and they fill the other one's goals: his crowns build her castle, hers build towards GTA 6.
- **Lore:** quotes, moments, photos and voice notes, numbered from No. 001. Voice notes can come from a screen recording; only the sound is kept, trimmed.
- **Timeline:** your dates, monthly and yearly anniversaries, "together for…", and the first time in the same room (date to be decided).
- **The Vault** (`#/vault`): its own quiet room. Letters with text, photos and voice notes, sealed until a date, until "Open when…", until the first time in the same room, or until the wedding day (both of you turn your keys). Truly sealed: only the envelope is visible until then. The writer is told when it's opened. Envelope fronts are a photo of your own hand-drawn one, or one of eight styles; letters come on writing paper, notebook paper or brown kraft, with a taped-in photo, little cards, and "take what you need" tear-off tabs (each torn once). "Open now!" letters open straight away.
- **The reveal** (`#/keys`): an official notice, the keys, a key of her own, and "Welcome home".

## Run it (PowerShell, one line at a time)

```
npm install
```
```
npm run dev
```

Without Supabase it's a demo saved in your browser, with a switch between his side and hers.
